Good questions

Before you roll

Can’t find your answer? Call us or send a message with the quote form.

Is real money involved?

No. Guests play with chips that have no cash value. Many hosts give prizes or raffle tickets to the top players.

How many tables do I need?

A good rule is one table for every 15 to 20 guests. We help you pick the right mix.

What areas do you serve?

San Antonio, Austin and nearby cities. Ask us about events farther away.

Do guests need to know how to play?

Not at all. Our dealers teach the games as they go, so beginners feel welcome.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to four weeks is ideal. Weekends in the holiday season fill up fast.
